Privacy stays on your side.

Plain-language summary: case content and review history remain in your browser. We do not run analytics or advertising trackers.

What the app stores

Your authored cases and review history are stored in IndexedDB on this device. A purchase license and its cached verification result are stored in localStorage. The app does not upload case content.

What leaves your device

If you buy or restore the one-time unlock, your browser connects to the Sociobot billing API to open hosted checkout or verify the license token. The verification request contains the token, not your cases. Sociobot/Dodo acts as merchant of record and handles payment information under its own notices.

Backups and deletion

Exported JSON backups go only to the download location you choose. Deleting a case also deletes its local review records. To erase everything, clear site data for this origin in your browser. Uninstalling the PWA may not clear browser storage on every platform.

Offline cache

The service worker caches the application shell and original generated illustration so the app opens offline. It contains no authored case data.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in. Please do not email confidential case content.
