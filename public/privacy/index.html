How Concept Case Bridge handles your data

Summary: case content and review history remain in your browser. We do not run analytics or advertising trackers.

What the app stores

Your cases and review history are stored in IndexedDB on this device. A license and its cached result use localStorage.

Demo cases use a separate IndexedDB database named demo:concept-case-bridge. Demo controls never read or change your real casebook.

What leaves your device

The app does not upload case content. It does not load analytics, advertising trackers, third-party fonts, or CDN scripts.

Buying or restoring a license connects to the Sociobot billing API. The verification request contains the license token, not your cases.

Sociobot/Dodo is the merchant of record and handles payment information under its own notices.

Backups and deletion

Exported JSON backups go to the download location you choose. Deleting a case also deletes its local reviews.

Clear this site’s browser data to erase everything. Uninstalling the PWA may not clear browser storage on every platform.

Offline cache

The service worker caches app files and the original illustration. It does not store authored cases.

Contact

Email privacy@sociobot.in with privacy questions. Do not email confidential case content.
